# Title: short present-tense imperative phrase (for example "Adopt consumer proxy")

> One ADR records **one** architecture decision. Keep it short, specific and dated.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Proposed / Accepted / Deprecated / Superseded by [ADR-XXXX](link) |
| **Date** | YYYY-MM-DD |
| **Deciders** | Names or roles |
| **Related SRS** | Link to the SRS and requirement IDs |

## Context

What problem forces a decision? Describe the situation, business priorities, constraints and the
forces in tension. Date-stamp anything that may change (costs, schedules, scale).

## Decision

State the decision in full sentences using the active voice: "We will ..."

## Consequences

What follows from the decision? Include positive, negative and neutral effects, new risks,
follow-up ADRs and when the decision will be reviewed.

## Alternatives considered (optional)

Briefly list the options that were rejected and why.
